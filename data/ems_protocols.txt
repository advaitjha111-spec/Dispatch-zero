PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-119
HAZMAT: ANHYDROUS AMMONIA (UN 1005)
SCENARIO: CHEMICAL SPILL OR LEAK
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill or leak area immediately for at least 100 meters (330 feet) in all directions.
2. Keep unauthorized personnel away. Stay upwind, uphill and/or upstream.
3. Ventilate closed spaces before entering, but only if wearing self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA).
HEALTH HAZARDS:
- TOXIC; may be fatal if inhaled, ingested or absorbed through skin.
- Contact with gas or liquefied gas may cause burns, severe injury and/or frostbite.
FIRE ACTIONS:
- Small Fire: Dry chemical or CO2.
- Large Fire: Water spray, fog or regular foam. Do not get water inside containers.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-128
HAZMAT: FLAMMABLE LIQUIDS (NON-POLAR / WATER-IMMISCIBLE)
SCENARIO: HIGHWAY TRANSPORT FIRE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill or leak area for at least 50 meters (150 feet) in all directions.
2. Eliminate all ignition sources (no smoking, flares, sparks, or flames in immediate area).
3. All equipment used when handling the product must be grounded.
FIRE ACTIONS:
- Small Fire: Dry chemical, CO2, water spray or regular foam.
- Large Fire: Water spray, fog or regular foam. Use water spray or fog; do not use straight streams.
- Move containers from fire area if you can do it without risk.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-CPR-01
SCENARIO: ADULT CARDIAC ARREST (NO PULSE, NOT BREATHING)
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Dispatch nearest ALS (Advanced Life Support) unit immediately.
2. Instruct the bystander to place the patient flat on their back on a hard surface.
3. Instruct the bystander to begin chest compressions immediately: Push hard and fast in the center of the chest (100 to 120 compressions per minute).
4. Depth of compressions should be at least 2 inches (5 cm).
5. Do not stop compressions to check for a pulse. Continue until the ALS unit arrives or an AED is ready to analyze.
