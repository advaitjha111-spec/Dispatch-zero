PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-119
HAZMAT: ANHYDROUS AMMONIA (UN 1005)
SCENARIO: CHEMICAL SPILL OR LEAK
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill or leak area immediately for at least 100 meters (330 feet) in all directions.
2. Keep unauthorized personnel away. Stay upwind, uphill and/or upstream.
3. Ventilate closed spaces before entering, but only if wearing self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA).
HEALTH HAZARDS:
- TOXIC; may be fatal if inhaled, ingested or absorbed through skin.
- Contact with gas or liquefied gas may cause burns, severe injury and/or frostbite.
FIRE ACTIONS:
- Small Fire: Dry chemical or CO2.
- Large Fire: Water spray, fog or regular foam. Do not get water inside containers.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-128
HAZMAT: FLAMMABLE LIQUIDS (NON-POLAR / WATER-IMMISCIBLE)
SCENARIO: HIGHWAY TRANSPORT FIRE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill or leak area for at least 50 meters (150 feet) in all directions.
2. Eliminate all ignition sources (no smoking, flares, sparks, or flames in immediate area).
3. All equipment used when handling the product must be grounded.
FIRE ACTIONS:
- Small Fire: Dry chemical, CO2, water spray or regular foam.
- Large Fire: Water spray, fog or regular foam. Use water spray or fog; do not use straight streams.
- Move containers from fire area if you can do it without risk.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-CPR-01
SCENARIO: ADULT CARDIAC ARREST (NO PULSE, NOT BREATHING)
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Dispatch nearest ALS (Advanced Life Support) unit immediately.
2. Instruct the bystander to place the patient flat on their back on a hard surface.
3. Instruct the bystander to begin chest compressions immediately: Push hard and fast in the center of the chest (100 to 120 compressions per minute).
4. Depth of compressions should be at least 2 inches (5 cm).
5. Do not stop compressions to check for a pulse. Continue until the ALS unit arrives or an AED is ready to analyze.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-111
HAZMAT: MIXED CHEMICAL SPILLS - GENERAL
SCENARIO: UNKNOWN HAZARDOUS MATERIAL RELEASE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill or leak area immediately for at least 50 meters in all directions.
2. Deny entry to unauthorized personnel and call specialized hazmat teams.
3. Stay clear of vapors, smoke, and associated runoff.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-112
HAZMAT: EXPLOSIVES - DIVISION 1.1 / 1.2
SCENARIO: EXPLOSIVE DEVICE OR CARGO FIRE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Evacuate all personnel for 800 meters (1/2 mile) in all directions if fire involves cargo.
2. Do not fight fire when it reaches explosives.
3. Isolate the danger zone and set up road blocks immediately.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-127
HAZMAT: FLAMMABLE LIQUIDS (POLAR / WATER-MISCIBLE)
SCENARIO: ALCOHOL OR SOLVENT SPILL FIRE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Isolate spill area for at least 50 meters.
2. Use alcohol-resistant foam, carbon dioxide, or dry chemical for small fires.
3. Prevent runoff from entering drains or water systems.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-135
HAZMAT: SUBSTANCES - SPONTANEOUSLY COMBUSTIBLE
SCENARIO: PYROPHORIC METAL OR CHEMICAL REACTIVE FIRE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Do not use water directly on burning material.
2. Use dry sand, limestone powder, or dry graphite to smother flames.
3. Isolate area for at least 100 meters.

PROTOCOL ID: DOT-ERG-153
HAZMAT: TOXIC AND/OR CORROSIVE SUBSTANCES (COMBUSTIBLE)
SCENARIO: ACID OR CAUSTIC LEAK WITH FIRE RISK
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Wear full protective gear with positive-pressure SCBA.
2. Stop leak if safe to do so without environmental exposure.
3. Neutralize liquid carefully with lime or soda ash if authorized.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-CPR-01
SCENARIO: ADULT CARDIAC ARREST
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Confirm unresponsiveness and absence of normal breathing.
2. Initiate continuous chest compressions at 100-120 bpm, depth at least 2 inches.
3. Apply AED immediately when available and follow audio prompts.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-CPR-02
SCENARIO: PEDIATRIC CARDIAC ARREST (INFANT)
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Deliver 15 chest compressions followed by 2 rescue breaths if trained.
2. Use two fingers for single rescuer; compress one-third the depth of the chest (approx 1.5 inches).
3. Call for advanced pediatric life support transport.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-AIRWAY-01
SCENARIO: SEVERE AIRWAY OBSTRUCTION / CHOKING (CONSCIOUS ADULT)
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Stand behind the patient and wrap arms around the waist.
2. Perform abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) inward and upward.
3. Continue until object is expelled or patient becomes unconscious.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-TRAUMA-01
SCENARIO: MASSIVE HEMORRHAGE / SEVERE BLEEDING
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Apply direct manual pressure with sterile gauze to the wound site.
2. If bleeding persists on an extremity, apply a tourniquet 2-3 inches above the wound.
3. Note the exact time the tourniquet was applied and secure it tightly.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-TRAUMA-02
SCENARIO: SUSPECTED CERVICAL SPINE INJURY
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Maintain manual in-line stabilization of the head and neck immediately.
2. Apply a rigid cervical collar of appropriate size.
3. Secure the patient onto a backboard with head blocks and straps.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-MED-01
SCENARIO: ANAPHYLACTIC SHOCK / SEVERE ALLERGIC REACTION
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Administer Epinephrine (0.3mg intramuscular) into the lateral thigh immediately.
2. Maintain airway patency and administer high-flow oxygen via non-rebreather mask.
3. Monitor vital signs every 5 minutes for signs of cardiovascular collapse.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-MED-02
SCENARIO: ACUTE ASTHMA EXACERBATION / BRONCHOSPASM
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Administer Albuterol via nebulizer or metered-dose inhaler.
2. Position patient upright in a position of comfort to maximize lung expansion.
3. Re-evaluate respiratory rate and oxygen saturation after 5 minutes.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-MED-03
SCENARIO: SUSPECTED STROKE / CEREBROVASCULAR ACCIDENT
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Perform FAST assessment (Face drooping, Arm drift, Speech slurred, Time to call).
2. Record exact time of symptom onset or last known normal state.
3. Keep patient NPO (nothing by mouth) and transport to a stroke-certified center.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-MED-04
SCENARIO: DIABETIC HYPOGLYCEMIA (LOW BLOOD GLUCOSE)
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Administer oral glucose gel or fast-acting carbohydrates if conscious and able to swallow.
2. If unconscious or unable to protect airway, administer Glucagon IM or establish IV dextrose.
3. Re-check blood glucose levels after 10 minutes.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-MED-05
SCENARIO: GENERALIZED TONIC-CLONIC SEIZURE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Clear surrounding area of hard or sharp objects to prevent physical injury.
2. Do not insert any objects into the patient's mouth or restrain limbs.
3. Place patient in recovery position post-seizure and administer oxygen.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-ENV-01
SCENARIO: HEAT STROKE / HYPERTHERMIA
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Move patient immediately to a shaded or air-conditioned environment.
2. Remove excess clothing and apply active cooling (ice packs to groin, axillae, neck, cold water immersion).
3. Monitor core temperature and prepare for rapid fluid resuscitation.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-ENV-02
SCENARIO: HYPOTHERMIA / SYSTEMIC COLD EXPOSURE
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Handle patient extremely gently to avoid triggering ventricular fibrillation.
2. Remove wet clothing and wrap in warm, dry blankets.
3. Apply passive external warming; avoid active rapid external warming of extremities.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-TOX-01
SCENARIO: OPIOID OVERDOSE / RESPIRATORY DEPRESSION
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Support ventilation with bag-valve-mask (BVM) and supplemental oxygen.
2. Administer Naloxone (Narcan) intranasally (2mg to 4mg) or intramuscularly.
3. Prepare to repeat dose if no respiratory response within 3 to 5 minutes.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-TOX-02
SCENARIO: CARBON MONOXIDE POISONING
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Immediately evacuate patient to fresh air environment.
2. Administer 100% oxygen via a non-rebreather mask with a tight seal.
3. Monitor carboxyhemoglobin levels and transport for hyperbaric evaluation if indicated.

PROTOCOL ID: EMS-OB-01
SCENARIO: EMERGENCY CHILDBIRTH / PRECIPITOUS DELIVERY
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Position mother on her back with knees bent and legs apart on a clean surface.
2. Support baby's head gently as it crowns; do not pull or tug on the infant.
3. Check for umbilical cord wrapped around the neck; gently slip it over the head if present.
